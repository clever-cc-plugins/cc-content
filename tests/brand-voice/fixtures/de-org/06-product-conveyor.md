# Modular conveyor line KX-300

The KX-300 is our standard modular conveyor line for mid-sized plants. It moves loads of up to 300 kilograms per metre and runs in two-shift operation without special maintenance.

## What it is

The line consists of straight sections, curves and transfer units. You choose the length and layout. We build it in our workshop in Paderborn, test it for 48 hours and deliver it ready to install.

## Key data

- Load capacity: up to 300 kg per metre
- Speed: 0.1 to 1.2 metres per second
- Section length: 2, 3 or 4 metres
- Drive: geared motor with frequency converter
- Material: galvanised steel or stainless steel

## Why companies choose it

First, it is simple. There are few parts, and they are standard parts. Your own technicians can replace most of them. Second, it can grow. If your volume increases, we add sections without rebuilding the line. Third, it is documented: every section has a number, and every number has a record.

## What it does not do

The KX-300 is not a sorting system. It does not identify products, and it does not control inventory. If you need that, we connect it to your warehouse software through a standard interface.

## Delivery and service

Standard lines are delivered within eight to twelve weeks. Spare parts are kept in stock for 15 years. Maintenance contracts cover two inspections per year and a 24-hour response time.
