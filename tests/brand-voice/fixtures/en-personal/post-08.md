Let's talk about the "quick call." It's never quick, and it's rarely a call.

I tracked mine for a month. Seventeen "quick calls." Average length: 41 minutes. Number that could've been a two-line message: twelve. I'm not complaining about the people. I like them. I'm complaining about me, because I said yes to all seventeen without thinking.

My new approach is boring. When someone asks for a quick call, I ask what they need to decide, and I answer in writing first. About half the time, that's enough. When it isn't, we have the call, and it's genuinely short, because we've both read the same thing.

Is it slightly less friendly? Maybe. But I'd rather be a little less available and a lot more useful.

One more observation: the people who ask for quick calls are rarely trying to waste my time. They're anxious, or they're stuck, and talking feels faster than writing. I get that. I just want us to be stuck for less time, together.
