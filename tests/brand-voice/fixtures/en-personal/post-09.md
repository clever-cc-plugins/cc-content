Small thing that changed how I write: I read my posts out loud before publishing.

It's embarrassing. I do it in the kitchen, and my partner has stopped asking. But it works. You hear the sentence that's too long. You hear the word you'd never actually say. You hear the moment you start sounding like a press release, which, trust me, happens fast.

If I stumble over a line, I cut it. If I'd feel silly saying it to a friend, I cut that too. What's left tends to be shorter, and, I think, more honest.

I'm not saying it makes me a great writer. I'm saying it makes me a slightly less annoying one.

Do you read your drafts out loud, or is that just me and the kitchen?

Bonus tip, borrowed from a radio producer I know: read the last sentence first. If it works on its own, the post probably has a point. If it doesn't, you've got a nice paragraph of fog and a nicer title.
